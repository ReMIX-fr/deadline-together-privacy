Deadline Together Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Overview

Deadline Together is designed to help users organize important dates, deadlines, reminders, meetings, groups, notes, and useful links.

Data Collection

Deadline Together does not collect, sell, share, or transfer personal data to third parties.

Local Data Storage

The app stores deadlines, notes, groups, reminders, links, meetings, preferences, and language settings locally on the user's device.

Notifications

The app may request notification permission in order to send deadline reminders selected by the user.

Calendar Access

The app may request calendar access only when the user chooses to add a deadline or meeting to the iOS Calendar.

Sharing

Users may choose to share deadlines or group invitations through external apps such as Messages, Mail, WhatsApp, or AirDrop. Deadline Together does not automatically share this information.

Third-Party Services

Deadline Together does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Deadline Together does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
